# Long-Term Project Roadmap: Income Engines & Self-Publishing Architecture

This roadmap establishes the phased milestones for testing, building, and deploying income assets without disrupting academic research.

---

## Phase 0: Defense & Survival Foundation (Immediate)
*Goal: Secure predictable runway so creative asset-building carries zero survival anxiety.*

- [ ] **Inventory Immediate Skills**: Identify 1–2 research-aligned high-probability freelance services (manuscript formatting, LaTeX styling, technical proofreading, literature synthesis).
- [ ] **Set Low-Burn Daily Minimums**: Allocate 45–60 minutes maximum per day to project assets; reserve peak cognitive hours for primary research.
- [ ] **Halt High-Burn/Low-Yield Tasks**: Freeze active uploads to TeePublic and Displate.

---

## Phase 1: Prototype & Market Validation (Weeks 1–3)
*Goal: Validate audience pull and production feasibility using the least amount of labor.*

### Workstream 1.1: The Dream Journal Novel & Visual Engine
- [ ] **Chapter 1 & 2 Polish**: Finalize prose for the first 2 chapters (approx. 2,500–4,000 words).
- [ ] **ComfyUI Asset Workflow**:
  - Establish a reproducible workflow (SDXL/Flux + ControlNet/IP-Adapter) for consistent surreal/dream aesthetic.
  - Generate 6–8 atmospheric visual assets for Chapter 1.
- [ ] **Pilot Short-Form Video**:
  - Script a 40–45s micro-story following the 4-part structure (Hook $\to$ Scene $\to$ Cliffhanger $\to$ Soft CTA).
  - Produce one clean 9:16 vertical MP4 (no platform watermarks).
  - Cross-post natively to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, and Reddit.
  - Measure audience retention, comments, and engagement.

### Workstream 1.2: Programmatic & Utility Niche Experiments
- [ ] **Lucid Dreaming / Sleep Architecture Logbook**:
  - Draft interior layout: reality check triggers, sleep cycle tracking, dream classification matrix.
  - Format as an 8.5" × 11" or 6" × 9" standard B&W interior PDF.
  - Calculate unit economics on KDP ($9.99 retail $\to$ ~$3.50 net royalty).
- [ ] **Digital Printable Ephemera (Etsy/Gumroad Prototype)**:
  - Select 1 tight theme (e.g., *Vintage Cartography & Travel Ephemera* or *Dark Surrealist Collage Pack*).
  - Assemble 15–20 high-res printable sheets (300 DPI, vector/clean cut lines).
  - Package as a digital download bundle (zero print overhead).

---

## Phase 2: Production & Scaling (Weeks 4–8)
*Goal: Turn winning prototypes into automated, recurring catalogs.*

- [ ] **Complete First Physical/Digital Launch**:
  - Launch either the completed novella or the specialized logbook on KDP.
  - Enable Kindle Unlimited (KDP Select) for eBook page reads.
- [ ] **Short-Form Content Batching**:
  - Establish a routine of 2–3 micro-story videos per week using modular ComfyUI asset presets.
- [ ] **Automate Interior Generation**:
  - Build simple Python scripts for generating puzzles, tabular logs, and formatted interior margins automatically.

---

## Phase 3: Brand & Long-Tail Systems (Month 3+)
*Goal: Compounding backlist with self-sustaining traffic.*

- [ ] Connect multi-book backlist (e.g., Book 1 feeds into Book 2).
- [ ] Explore long-form YouTube video essays (8–12 minutes) exploring dream lore or creative technology deep-dives.
- [ ] Build an email newsletter list via free printable or sample chapters to own the customer relationship directly.
