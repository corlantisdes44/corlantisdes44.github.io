## Core Operational Principles

1. **Survival First, Assets Second**: Protect cognitive research energy with reliable, immediate exchange-of-service income before relying on algorithmic platforms.
2. **Strict Black & White for KDP**: Avoid full-color paperback printing on Amazon KDP (which destroys margins).
3. **Etsy/Gumroad for Visual Ephemera**: Never sell thin paper cutout pads on KDP; sell digital print-at-home PDF bundles instead.
4. **Owned Organic Distribution**: Treat Amazon as a fulfillment cart; drive discovery via clean, watermark-free vertical micro-story videos cross-posted to YouTube Shorts, Reels, X, and Reddit.
